# Load-Bearing Bracket Design

## Overview
Designed a mass-minimized structural bracket to support known loads, validated through hand calculations, FEA, and physical testing.

## Tools
CAD, Solid Mechanics, FEA, Manual Machining

## Problem Definition
The bracket was required to support specified loads while meeting factor of safety and manufacturing constraints.

## Hand Analysis
Calculated stresses and factors of safety under worst-case loading conditions to size the bracket geometry.

## CAD Design
<img src="/assets/images/bracket/bracket-cad.png" alt="CAD model of load-bearing bracket" width="500">

*Figure 1. SolidWorks CAD model of the load-bearing bracket used for hand calculations and FEA.*

Modeled the bracket geometry in CAD, incorporating fillets and thickness variations to reduce stress concentrations.

## Finite Element Analysis
<img src="/assets/images/bracket/bracket-fea.png" alt="Finite element stress analysis of load-bearing bracket under applied load" width="500">

*Figure 2. Finite element von Mises stress distribution for the load-bearing bracket under the design load, used to verify stress levels and factor of safety.*

Performed FEA to validate stress distributions and compare results against hand calculations.

## Fabrication & Testing
Fabricated the bracket using manual machining techniques and tested under applied loads.

## Results & Iteration
Testing informed design iterations to improve performance and reduce mass.

## Reflection
This project strengthened my understanding of structural validation and iterative design.
